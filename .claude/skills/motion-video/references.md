Studied for structure and craft only. Nothing here may be copied: no branding, layouts, assets,
footage or wording. Method: downloaded at 720p, cuts found with ffmpeg scene detection (score above
0.18), one frame per shot (or 24 evenly spaced frames for continuous pieces) on contact sheets, and
dense strips around chosen transitions.

## 1. Dark release teaser (30 s, one continuous take)
- Opens on the product's data drawn as thin lines converging into one path, extreme close-up,
  shallow depth of field, slow drift for about 5 s. No UI yet: the idea before the interface.
- Pulls back to the same structure on a plane tilted in perspective.
- Cut to black with a centered monospace caps caption, wide letter-spacing; it sharpens in, holds
  about 2 s, then dissolves while the next scene rises behind it.
- Several panels stacked in depth slide up one after another.
- Closing: a typewriter caption with a block cursor types a word, erases, types the call to
  action; the logo holds about 3 s.
- Monochrome with one muted accent; calm, about 5 s per beat.

## 2 and 3. Narrated walkthroughs (screen recordings)
- Real UI as a window with a soft shadow on a light background, a presenter in a corner bubble.
- When detail matters the frame zooms in on that region, then back out.
- Useful for the zoom-to-region recipe; otherwise not motion design.

## 4. Launcher teaser (39 s)
- Each beat is one UI element at macro scale, cropped off the frame edge, on an out-of-focus
  monochrome background of soft folds; a slow push-in during each shot.
- Typing builds content inside the element (caret, about 10 characters a second, results appear
  below as you type).
- One element per beat, about 1.5 to 3 s each; light sweeps across the background between beats.
- Ends with tiny monospace caps in two columns ("NEW" | feature name), several in quick
  succession, then the logo alone.

## 5. Launcher announcement (299 s)
- Presenter to camera, intercut with macro UI shots and one abstract "warp" transition into a
  screen. The rhythm comes from alternating face and product.

## 6. Launcher subscription launch (130 s)
- Montage: a bold centered feature word on a blurred colour bloom (about 0.8 s) alternates with a
  UI clip of that feature (about 1.4 s), cut on the beat.
- UI clips show a change happening (a theme switching, a list filtering), not a still screen.
- The climax title gets a brighter, bigger bloom; then the presenter explains.

## 7. Agency showreel (55 s)
- Kinetic type: a word enters small, overshoots to fill the frame with a perspective skew, settles;
  the next phrase arrives letter by letter.
- Number reveal: the figure enters huge and blurred, sharpens as it shrinks, then its meaning
  slides in beside it in an accent colour.
- A word roller: a fixed word plus a vertical list of alternatives passing a highlight.
- UI cards float in 3D with glow on dark gradients; light and dark scenes alternate.
- Very fast (quarter of shots under 0.7 s): impressive but often too quick to read; Brimline's
  audience needs to read, so this pace is used for word cards only.
